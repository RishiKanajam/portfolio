# Portfolio design

User-approved direction: a well-set document. On wide screens (≥960px) a 1120px page with section headings in a sticky left column and content on the right; reading measure stays ≤680px / 64ch; one column on phones. White and near-black with a matching dark theme. IBM Plex Sans for prose and headings; JetBrains Mono only for status, dates and metadata. Underlined text links, generous section spacing, no cards, shadows or gradients. Dividers are dotted rules (--dot) in the hero field's point language; each one sits blurred until it scrolls into view, then comes into focus (FocusObserver; sharp without JS or with reduced motion). Motion is limited to the hero field, a 0.25em arrow nudge and underline colour on hover. Respect system theme with an optional persisted override. Home prioritises selected work, more work, experience, then two original About paragraphs. Full prose lives on static project pages and the background page.

Hierarchy: name 2.25–2.75rem; thesis set larger (1.25–1.5rem, weight 500) as the page's one statement; section headings 1.375rem; project titles at body size, semibold. Secondary text (role, org, project one-liners, "One decision:" label) uses the muted token. Link underlines are a translucent ink tone that turns solid on hover. Experience rows put role and organisation on the left with mono dates right-aligned, which wrap below on narrow screens.

Hero field (three.js, user-approved): a monochrome landscape of points seen through a lens with real depth of field. The focal distance follows the pointer (raycast onto the ground plane): points at that distance are small and sharp, nearer and farther points blur into soft discs. It acts out the thesis, in focus where there's evidence. Drawn in --ink so it follows the theme; masked to fade before the first section. three.js is dynamically imported on first interaction or 4s after load, never blocks first paint, pauses off-screen and in hidden tabs, and renders one still frame under prefers-reduced-motion. On touch, focus drifts near and far on its own.

Home structure: big name (≤6rem, -0.04em) and two-line thesis anchored to the bottom of a near-full-height hero; Selected work rows with a mono meta column (status, award, credit, top three stack items) beside title, summary, one decision and links; More work as a two-column hairline grid; Experience as date | role/org rows; About with a larger lede; footer with the existing closing line set large, plus email.

Signature, carried through the page: "it tells you how much to trust it". Each featured project has an evidence ledger in its left column: Evidence (filled dot, things a reader can check), Limits (hollow dot, what isn't done or proven yet) and Built with. Ledger lines only restate facts already in that project's entry; never add new claims there. Under "Selected work" the sticky left column carries a live index of the four projects (ProjectIndex) that marks the one being read. Experience is a dotted vertical timeline on ≥720px, with filled markers for current roles and hollow markers for past ones.
